# Alpha Research Platform (ARP)

## Vision

Alpha Research Platform (ARP) is an AI-powered Business Intelligence platform for ecommerce.

Its mission is to help entrepreneurs, brands, and ecommerce businesses discover, evaluate, and validate profitable business opportunities using data-driven analysis instead of intuition.

ARP is designed as a long-term platform rather than a single-purpose application.

The platform will continuously integrate new marketplaces, supplier networks, AI capabilities, automation workflows, and analytical engines without requiring architectural redesign.

Every recommendation generated by the platform must be explainable, transparent, and supported by measurable data.

The long-term vision is to build an intelligent operating system for ecommerce decision-making.

## Design Principles

### 1. Platform First
ARP is designed as a long-term platform, not a single-purpose application. Every new capability should integrate into the platform without requiring architectural redesign.

### 2. Data Before AI
Artificial Intelligence assists with interpretation and explanation, but every recommendation must be based on structured, measurable data.

### 3. Explainable Decisions
Every recommendation must include the evidence, metrics, and reasoning that produced it. No "black box" decisions.

### 4. Modular Architecture
Every major capability must exist as an independent module or engine with clear responsibilities and minimal coupling.

### 5. Replaceable Components
External providers, AI models, databases, marketplaces, and supplier integrations must be replaceable without affecting the rest of the system.

### 6. API First
All core business capabilities should be accessible through internal APIs to support future applications, automation, and third-party integrations.

### 7. Single Responsibility
Each module, service, connector, and engine should have one clearly defined responsibility.

### 8. Scalable by Design
Scalability must be considered from the beginning. New marketplaces, supplier networks, AI models, and analytical engines should be added as extensions rather than requiring system redesign.

### 9. Documentation Before Implementation
Architecture, workflows, and business rules must be documented before implementation begins.

### 10. Business Value First
Every feature must solve a real business problem or improve decision quality. Features without measurable value should not be implemented.

## High-Level Architecture

Alpha Research Platform follows a Modular Monorepo Architecture.

The system is organized into independent domains and engines, each with a single responsibility and well-defined interfaces.

The architecture is designed around the following layers:

- Presentation Layer
- Application Layer
- Domain Layer
- Infrastructure Layer
- External Integration Layer

Frontend and Backend evolve independently while sharing common domain models and contracts.

All external systems, including AI providers, marketplaces, supplier networks, databases, and third-party services, are accessed through adapters to prevent vendor lock-in.

Business logic must never depend directly on external providers.

The architecture is designed to support future distributed services without requiring major redesign.

## Core Modules

The platform is composed of the following major components:

### 1. Web Application
Provides the user interface for research, analysis, dashboards, reports, and project management.

### 2. API Backend
Exposes all business capabilities through internal APIs and coordinates application workflows.

### 3. Research Engine
Collects product and marketplace data through marketplace connectors.

### 4. Supply Engine
Collects supplier information and evaluates supply chain opportunities.

### 5. Normalization Engine
Transforms data from different external sources into a unified internal model.

### 6. Analytics Engine
Calculates business metrics, opportunity scores, trends, competition indicators, and profitability insights.

### 7. AI Engine
Generates explanations, summaries, recommendations, and business insights based on structured analytical results.

### 8. Decision Engine
Combines analytical results and AI insights to produce explainable business recommendations.

### 9. Reporting Engine
Builds dashboards, reports, exports, and business summaries.

### 10. Connector Layer
Provides adapters for marketplaces, supplier networks, AI providers, and future third-party integrations.

### 11. Data Platform
Stores projects, products, analyses, suppliers, reports, historical data, and application configuration.

Each component should communicate through clearly defined interfaces and remain independently replaceable whenever possible.

## Data Flow

The platform processes information through a sequential business pipeline.

1. The user creates or opens a research project.

2. The Research Engine collects marketplace data.

3. The Supply Engine collects supplier information.

4. The Normalization Engine transforms all external data into the internal domain model.

5. The Analytics Engine calculates business metrics, opportunity scores, trends, competition analysis, and profitability indicators.

6. The AI Engine analyzes the structured analytical results and generates explanations, summaries, and business insights.

7. The Decision Engine combines analytical results and AI reasoning to produce explainable recommendations.

8. The Reporting Engine generates dashboards, reports, exports, and historical records.

9. Results are presented to the user through the Web Application.

Raw external data should never bypass the Normalization Engine.

Business decisions should always be based on normalized and structured data.
