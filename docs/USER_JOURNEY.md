# Target User

The first version of Alpha Research Platform targets independent ecommerce entrepreneurs and small businesses looking to discover profitable product opportunities.

Primary users include:

- Amazon sellers
- eBay sellers
- Etsy sellers
- Shopify store owners
- WooCommerce store owners
- Product researchers
- Ecommerce consultants

These users are not data scientists.

They need actionable business recommendations instead of raw analytics.

The platform should prioritize simplicity, explainability, and decision support over technical complexity.

# User Goals

## Primary Goals

- Discover profitable ecommerce opportunities.
- Reduce manual product research time.
- Compare multiple marketplaces in one workflow.
- Receive explainable AI-backed recommendations.
- Save and revisit research projects.

## Secondary Goals

- Monitor market trends.
- Compare supply sources.
- Export research reports.
- Prepare findings for collaboration in future versions.
